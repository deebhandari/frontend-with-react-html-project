Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Chairly.

What is Chairly? −

Chairly is an all-in-one platform designed to help beauty businesses manage appointments, clients, payments and business operations.

How does the scheduling system work? +

Clients can book appointments while businesses manage their availability through the calendar.

Can my clients book online? +

Yes. Online booking allows clients to view available services and appointment times.

Can I manage multiple staff members? +

Yes. Staff schedules, services and appointments can be managed from the same platform.

Is there a free trial? +

The platform can offer a free trial depending on the selected plan.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Subscription management is available from your account settings.
